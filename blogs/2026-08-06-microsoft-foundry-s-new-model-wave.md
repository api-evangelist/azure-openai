The era of the single best model is over Microsoft Foundry is no longer just a shelf of foundation models. It is becoming an enterprise model portfolio: frontier models for complex reasoning, cost-optimized models for scale, specialized models for documents and media, and first-party Microsoft AI models for text, image, voice, and speech. That shift changes the operating question for customers and partners.
