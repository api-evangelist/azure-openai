On-Device AI Inference Changes the Architecture, Not the Risk As organizations transform into Frontier Firms and integrate AI into more applications, cloud-hosted inference is not always the optimal architectural choice. Some applications operate with limited connectivity, require low-latency responses, or involve data that organizations prefer to process locally. As AI usage scales, token economics also becomes an architectural consideration as organizations look for greater predictability and control over cloud inference cost.
