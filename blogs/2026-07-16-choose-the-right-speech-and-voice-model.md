Choose the right speech and voice model in Microsoft Foundry The practical approach to selecting the right model is simple: do not ask which speech model is best. Ask what the speech system must guarantee. Once you know whether the job is conversation, translation, transcription, diarization, synthesis, or managed voice-agent orchestration, the model choice becomes much clearer.
