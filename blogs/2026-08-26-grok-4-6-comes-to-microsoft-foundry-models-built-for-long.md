The next wave of AI applications won't be defined by how quickly models answer questions. They'll be defined by how much work they can complete. Whether resolving repository issues, navigating codebases, generating engineering designs, or executing multi-step enterprise workflows, developers are increasingly building systems that need models capable of staying on task across complex, long-running objectives.
