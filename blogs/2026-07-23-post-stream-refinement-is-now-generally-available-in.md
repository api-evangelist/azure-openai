When we introduced Post-Stream Refinement in public preview earlier this year, it closed the oldest trade-off in real-time speech: you could finally keep instant streaming results and get a highly accurate final transcript, with no penalty to first-token latency. A second recognition pass runs in parallel with streaming and replaces each final segment with a more accurate version once the utterance completes. Today, Post-Stream Refinement reaches general availability for Azure AI Speech in Microsoft Foundry, backed by a production SLA.
